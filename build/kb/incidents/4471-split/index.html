The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is what #4471 describes, and the queued fix is for this one. It covers reports from the app, email, and the booking-v2 form.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY, people follow it, and the form reads what they typed the tenant’s way, day first. Only form reports from the booking-v3 tenants have it. The parser fix does not touch this path: the form already reads day first. The fix is the hint, or reading the field the way the hint asks. #4471’s own table marks this group “not this ticket — no ticket of its own”.

one-day: dates move by one day, not by a month. A meeting on Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and one in Toronto gets Monday; an invite arrives before the booking was made. No month/day flip moves a date by one day, so neither fix above closes these. The desk already read them this way (desk-notes.json) and the #4471 table footnotes them, but counts them under parser. They point at time zones or a date conversion across them.

How the numbers are made

  • The export has repeated rows, because pages overlap; each report counts once by id.
  • parser and hint come from causeOf in causes.ts: tenant locale and booking form, never intake’s tag. Intake’s tag disagrees with the cause in both directions.
  • one-day is the desk’s list in desk-notes.json, taken out before causeOf runs.
  • The table recomputes all of it from the export on every build. If the desk merges more reports into #4471, the numbers move with it; the words here do not need to.
